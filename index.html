🌙 Iftar Ideas

Simple iftar plates that work for a busy family — start with dates and water, then soup, then the main.

🌰

Dates + Water

The Sunnah way to break the fast. 2–3 dates, a glass of water, then pray Maghrib before the main meal.

🍲

Lentil Soup (Shorba)

Red lentils, onion, cumin, lemon. Light, warm and easy on an empty stomach — ready in 25 minutes.

🥗

Fruit Chaat

Apples, bananas, oranges, grapes with a pinch of chaat masala and lemon. Sweet, fresh, and the kids love it.

🍗

Iftar Main: Butter Chicken

Make the butter chicken from the recipe below, with naan and rice. Cook the sauce earlier in the day — just warm and serve.
